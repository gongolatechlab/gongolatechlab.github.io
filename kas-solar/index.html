Engr Kabir Abdullah Shettima

Chief Executive Officer · KAS Solar Energy & Engineering Services Ltd

Engr Kabir Abdullah Shettima is an engineer and entrepreneur based in Yola, Adamawa State, with a career built on delivering practical engineering solutions to real energy problems in Northern Nigeria. Under his leadership, KAS Solar Energy & Engineering Services Ltd has grown into a trusted name for solar photovoltaic installations, system deployment and technical support across the state.

A firm believer in powering communities through clean energy, he is currently contesting for a seat in the House of Representatives in Adamawa State — carrying the same engineering discipline into public service.
